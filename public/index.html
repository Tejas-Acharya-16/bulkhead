    What is real here

    Real
    Every question is a real JEE Advanced question (2016–2023) from JEEBench, MIT licence. Decoys in the samples are real JEE questions that are not in the bank.
    Simulated
    The sample leaks: real questions with copying errors, cut-offs and rewording added by code.
    Assumed
    The demo exam: 60 questions per form, 8 print lines, 4 presses. The re-sit figure uses an illustrative 22 lakh candidates and equal line sizes.
    Built, with limits
    Reading photos (Tesseract OCR, in the browser). Formulas rarely survive OCR, so matching falls back on the words. Real handwriting is harder than the sample's handwriting-style font.

    A named line or press starts an investigation. It is not a finding that any person leaked the paper.
